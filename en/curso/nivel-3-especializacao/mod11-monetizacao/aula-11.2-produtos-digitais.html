Digital Products and Scalability

Create and sell digital products that generate passive income while you sleep.

⏱️ ~90 minutes 📊 Advanced 🔄 Updated Apr/2026
1

📚 E-books with AI Illustrations

E-books remain one of the most accessible digital products to create and sell. With AI, you can produce complete e-books with professional illustrations in a fraction of the time and cost traditionally required.

Types of profitable AI-generated e-books

Type Example Suggested price
Practical guide "How to create viral videos with AI in 2026" R$29-49
Illustrated children's book Stories with Midjourney/FLUX illustrations R$19-39
Prompt book Collection of tested prompts for Midjourney/Kling R$19-79
Coffee table book Themed AI art (landscapes, sci-fi, fantasy) R$49-99

E-book creation pipeline

  1. Set the topic and target audience
  2. Generate an outline with Claude/GPT
  3. Write the content (AI + human review)
  4. Create illustrations with Midjourney/FLUX
  5. Lay it out in Canva or InDesign
  6. Publish on Gumroad, Hotmart, or Amazon KDP

🎓 Online Courses on AI Tools

If you’ve mastered the tools taught in this VISION course, you have enough knowledge to create and sell your own courses on specific topics. The AI education market is growing explosively.

Most popular course topics

  • Midjourney for beginners: High demand, broad audience
  • Video creation with Kling/Runway: Growing niche
  • Automation with n8n for creators: High willingness to pay
  • Advanced ComfyUI workflows: Technical audience, high ticket
  • Music production with Suno: New niche with little competition

Platforms for selling courses

  • • Hotmart: Largest Brazilian platform. Robust affiliate program. Commission: ~10-15%
  • • Udemy: Massive global audience. Low prices but high volume. Commission: 37-63%
  • • Teachable: Full control over pricing and branding. Fixed monthly fee + small commission
  • • Gumroad: Simple and straightforward. Ideal for smaller courses. Commission: 10%

🎨 Template Packs: Scalable Passive Income

Template packs are extremely scalable digital products. You create them once and sell them infinitely. With VISION skills, you can create templates for various tools and niches.

✍️ Prompt Packs (Midjourney/FLUX)

Collections of 50-200 tested prompts for specific niches (photography, architecture, fashion). Price: R$19-49.

🔧 ComfyUI workflows

Ready-made workflows for upscaling, inpainting, and style transfer. Technical audiences pay more. Price: R$29-99.

✂️ CapCut Templates

Editing templates for Reels, TikToks, and Shorts. High demand among social media managers. Price: R$19-39.

⚙️ n8n/Make Workflows

Ready-to-use automations (auto-posting, newsletter, analytics). High perceived value. Price: R$49-199.

📸 Stock Media: Sell AI Images and Videos

The stock media market is undergoing an AI-driven transformation. Platforms are accepting AI-generated content (subject to rules), and demand for unique, high-quality images and videos continues to grow.

Platform Does it accept AI? Pay per download
Adobe Stock ✅ With the "AI-generated" tag $0.33-$0.66
Shutterstock ✅ Through a specific program $0.25-$0.38
Wirestock ✅ Multi-platform distribution Variable (aggregates several)
Freepik ✅ AI contributor program $0.05-$0.50

Volume and consistency

In stock media, profit comes from volume. A portfolio of 500-1,000 quality images in specific niches (business, technology, lifestyle) can generate $200-500/month in passive income. Use automated pipelines to generate and submit at scale.

💻 SaaS with Vibe Coding

With the vibe coding skills you learned in Level 2, you can create micro-SaaS (Software as a Service) products that solve specific problems for content creators. Even if you’re not a professional programmer, tools like v0, Replit, and Cursor let you build functional products.

Micro-SaaS ideas for creators

  • AI Thumbnail Generator: Upload a frame + generate 10 optimized thumbnail variations
  • Content planner: Editorial calendar with AI-powered topic suggestions
  • Script analyzer: Paste your script and get feedback on the hook, pacing, and CTA
  • Prompt library: Collaborative platform for saving, sharing, and selling prompts
  • Analytics dashboard: Consolidates YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok metrics in one place

SaaS business model

Offer a limited free plan (freemium) to attract users, and paid plans starting at $9-29/month. With 100 subscribers at $19/month, you have $1.900/month in recurring revenue. Scale with content marketing (which you already know how to do with AI).

🚀 Scaling Strategies

To maximize returns on your digital products, apply these scaling strategies:

  1. Start with 1 product: Validate demand before diversifying. Launch a minimum viable product and measure sales
  2. Build a funnel: Free product (lead magnet) → low-cost product (R$19-49) → premium product (R$99-299)
  3. Automate your marketing: Use the pipelines from Module 10 to promote automatically
  4. Affiliate program: On Hotmart/Gumroad, enable affiliates to sell for you (20-50% commission)
  5. Bundle and upsell: Bundle products with a discount. Offer an upgrade at checkout
  6. Iterate based on data: Use analytics to understand what sells and double down on the winners
