📣 Module 11 • Lesson 3

Content Marketing with AI

Master SEO, social media, email marketing, and paid advertising powered by artificial intelligence.

⏱️ ~90 minutes 📊 Advanced 🔄 Updated Apr/2026
1

🔍 AI-Powered SEO

Search engine optimization in 2026 is fundamentally different from what it was just a few years ago. With AI integrated into Google search results (SGE) and the rise of AI-powered search (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search), SEO strategy needs to adapt.

SEO with AI: The New Playbook

Strategy How to Implement with AI Tool
Keyword research AI analyzes competitors and suggests low-competition long-tail keywords SEMrush Copilot, Ahrefs AI
Content creation Outline + draft + H2/H3 and meta description optimization Claude, SurferSEO
On-page optimization Automated analysis of title tags, headers, and internal linking Clearscope, MarketMuse
Content for SGE Format content to appear in Google AI snippets Schema markup + AI
YouTube SEO AI-optimized titles, descriptions, and tags for each video vidIQ, TubeBuddy + LLM

SEO for AI Videos

YouTube videos are the second-largest search source in the world. Optimize titles with high-intent keywords, write descriptions of 200+ words with timestamps, and use relevant tags. With AI, you can generate all of this automatically in the publishing pipeline.

2

📱 Social Media Automation

A social media strategy in 2026 requires a presence on multiple platforms, with native content for each one. AI-powered automation is the only way to maintain that presence without a large team.

AI-powered multi-platform strategy

Instagram / TikTok

Daily Reels generated by a pipeline. Hook in the first 3s. AI-optimized hashtags. Automatic best time to post.

YouTube

Weekly Shorts + long-form videos. AI-tested A/B thumbnails. SEO-optimized descriptions. Automatic chapters.

LinkedIn

Professional posts 3x/week. Carousels with niche insights. Formal tone adapted by AI. Strategic networking.

X (Twitter)

Weekly threads + daily tweets. Hot takes on trends. Automated engagement with relevant mentions.

Repurposing with Opus Clip

Opus Clip analyzes long videos and automatically identifies the best moments to create viral short clips. Integrate it into your pipeline: record one long video per week → Opus Clip extracts 5-10 clips → the pipeline distributes them to all platforms with captions and native formats.

3

📧 Email Marketing with AI Personalization

Email remains the channel with the highest ROI in digital marketing. With AI, you can personalize each email individually, dramatically increasing open and conversion rates.

AI Email Strategies

  • Optimized subject lines: AI generates 10 variations and suggests the best one based on historical data
  • Dynamic customization: Email content changes based on reader behavior
  • Smart segmentation: AI automatically classifies leads by interest and funnel stage
  • Follow-up automation: Email sequences dynamically adjusted based on engagement
  • Curated newsletter: AI agent selects and summarizes relevant content each week
Tool Integrated AI Free plan
Mailchimp Subject line optimizer, send time optimization 500 contacts
ConvertKit AI writing assistant, smart segmentation 1,000 contacts
Beehiiv AI writer, recommendation engine 2,500 contacts
4

💰 Ads with AI-generated creative

Advertising platforms like Meta Ads and Google Ads are increasingly integrating AI. Creators who master AI creative generation have a significant competitive advantage: they can test more variations at a lower cost.

Creative pipeline for ads

  1. Generate 20 AI image/video variations (FLUX, Kling)
  2. Create 10 copy variations with an LLM (Claude/GPT)
  3. Create combinations at scale in the ads manager
  4. Let the platform's algorithm optimize (DCO - Dynamic Creative Optimization)
  5. Analyze results and feed learnings into the pipeline

Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook)

Advantage+ Creative uses native AI to optimize creative assets. Combine it with your AI-generated assets for maximum performance.

Google Ads

Performance Max automatically generates combinations of creative assets. Feed it high-quality AI-generated images and text.

5

👥 Building an Audience and Community

Tools and automations are means, not ends. Your true long-term asset is your audience and community. Here’s how to build that systematically:

Audience-building pillars

  1. Consistency: Publish at regular times. Use automation to stay consistent even when you’re busy
  2. Value first: 80% valuable content, 20% promotion. Teach, inspire, and entertain before selling
  3. Authenticity: Share your process, mistakes, and lessons learned. People connect with people, not perfection
  4. Engagement: Reply to comments (AI can help prioritize and draft responses). Participate in niche communities
  5. Cross-pollination: Direct your audience between platforms. YouTube → Newsletter → Community → Products
  6. Community: Create an exclusive space (Discord, Telegram group, Circle) where your most engaged fans can interact
