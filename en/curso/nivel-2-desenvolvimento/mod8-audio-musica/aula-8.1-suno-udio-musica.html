Suno v5.5 and Udio v4 — AI Music

Generate full songs with realistic vocals, professional music production, and video integration.

~120 minutes • Updated in April 2026

🎵 The AI-Generated Music Revolution

In 2026, AI music generation reached a level few had predicted. With more than 7 million songs created each day on Suno alone, AI has established itself as a legitimate music production tool. In this lesson, you’ll master the market’s two leading platforms: Suno v5 e Udio v4.

💡 Important Context

AI music generation is already being used in YouTube productions, podcasts, short film soundtracks, and even commercial music. Understanding these tools is essential for any audiovisual content creator.

🎶 Suno v5.5 — The State of the Art in AI Music

Launched in September 2025, Suno v5.5 marked a huge leap in quality. With 44.1kHz audio, extremely realistic vocals, and the ability to generate songs up to 4 minutes long, the platform became the industry benchmark.

🎤 Suno v5 Main Features

  • ✅ Audio in 44.1kHz — CD quality
  • ✅ Extremely realistic vocals with emotional nuances
  • ✅ Music up to 4 minutes of duration
  • ✅ 12 stems separated (vocals, drums, bass, guitar, etc.)
  • ✅ Vocal Personas — consistent voices across songs
  • ✅ Suno Studio — Built-in DAW for editing
  • ✅ More than 7 million songs created per day

Vocal Personas

One of the most revolutionary features of Suno v5.5 is Vocal Personas. You can create a "vocal persona" and reuse it in different songs while keeping the voice consistent. This is perfect for creating a "virtual artist" with its own sound identity.

Suno Studio (DAW)

Suno Studio is an integrated digital workstation that lets you edit your music directly on the platform. You can adjust the volume of each stem, rearrange sections, add effects, and export in high quality.

Versions and pricing

🎧 Udio v4 — Studio Quality and Long Context

Udio v4 is Suno’s main competitor and stands out for its superior audio quality (48kHz stereo), long context of up to 10 minutes, and advanced features like audio inpainting and genre remixing.

🎚️ Udio v4 Main Features

  • ✅ Audio in 48kHz stereo — superior quality
  • ✅ Context up to 10 minutes — ideal for long-form content
  • ✅ Stem separation integrated
  • ✅ Audio Inpainting — edit specific sections
  • ✅ Genre Remix — transform the genre of a song
  • ✅ Licensing with UMG + WMG + Kobalt

⚠️ ATTENTION: Downloads disabled

Since October 2025, Udio has disabled music downloads while it resolves licensing issues. You can create and listen to your music on the platform, but you can’t download it. Check the current status before starting a project that depends on exporting.

🛠️ Step by step: Creating your first song with AI

We'll create a song from scratch using Suno v5.5. This process is simple and takes less than 5 minutes.

📝 Step 1: Go to Suno

Access suno.com and log in. If this is your first time, create a free account. You'll receive 50 daily credits to try it out.

📝 Step 2: Set the Style

Click "Create" and choose the mode. In Simple, just describe what you want: "an upbeat pop song about traveling the world, female vocals, in Portuguese". In Custom, you can specify lyrics, genre, BPM, and more.

📝 Step 3: Configure the parameters

  • 🎵 Gender: Pop, Rock, Lo-fi, Sertanejo, MPB, etc.
  • 🎤 Vocal: Male, female, or instrumental
  • ⏱️ Duration: 1 to 4 minutes
  • 🌍 Language: Portuguese, English, or any language

📝 Step 4: Generate and iterate

Click "Create" and wait. Suno generates 2 variations by default. Listen to both and choose the best one. Use the "Extend" button to extend or "Remix" to create variations.

📝 Step 5: Export the stems

On the Premier plan, access Suno Studio and export up to 12 separate stems. This lets you mix the music in your video editor, adjusting vocal and instrument volumes independently.

🎬 Music for Video: Matching Mood, Tempo, and Style

Creating music for video requires attention to three fundamental elements: the mood (emotional tone), the time (BPM) and the style musical. Let’s explore how to align each one.

Mood (Emotional tone)

Video type Recommended mood Example prompt
Travel Vlog Cheerful, adventurous "upbeat indie folk, acoustic guitar, happy, travel vibes"
Documentary Introspective, epic "cinematic orchestral, emotional, documentary score"
Tutorial Neutral, light "lo-fi hip hop, calm, background music, study vibes"
Reels/Shorts Energetic, catchy "catchy pop beat, energetic, viral, 120 BPM"
Horror/Suspense Tense, mysterious "dark ambient, tension building, horror soundtrack"

Tempo (BPM)

BPM (beats per minute) defines the energy of the music. As a general rule:

  • 🐢 60–80 BPM: Slow, emotional, introspective scenes
  • 🚶 80–110 BPM: Moderate pace, conversations, vlogs
  • 🏃 110-140 BPM: Energetic, action, dance
  • ⚡ 140+ BPM: High energy, sports, fast-paced edits

💡 Pro Tip

When creating music for video, always generate versions instrumentals e with vocals. The instrumental version works as background music while you narrate, and the vocal version can be used for intros, transitions, or highlights.
