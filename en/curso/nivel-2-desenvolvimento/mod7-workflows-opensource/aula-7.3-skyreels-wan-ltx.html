SkyReels, Wan 2.7, and LTX-Video 2.3

Explore the most advanced open-source video generation models: SkyReels V4 with native audio, Wan 2.7 with first/last-frame control, and LTX-Video 2.3 with 4K at 50fps.

1

🌌 SkyReels — The Most Advanced Open Source

O SkyReels evolved rapidly since its first version, and V4 marks a historic moment: it’s the the first open-source model to generate video + audio simultaneously at 1080p at 32fps.

Version Innovation Resolution/FPS
SkyReels V2 First competitive version, diffusion-based 720p / 24fps
SkyReels V3 Best visual quality, longer clips 1080p / 24fps
SkyReels V4 First open-source model with native dual video and audio 1080p / 32fps

What does “dual nativo” mean?

SkyReels V4 generates video and audio in parallel during the same diffusion process, rather than as post-processing. This results in much better synchronization between sound and image — footsteps line up with footstep sounds, and falling objects make noise on the exact frame they hit.

2

🎭 Wan 2.7 — Alibaba's Giant

O Wan 2.7, developed by Alibaba, is a fully open-source model with 27 billion parameters. It is the open-source model with the most available control features:

  • • First/Last Frame Control — Define exactly how the video starts and ends; AI fills in the intermediate frames
  • • 5 Reference Videos — Use up to 5 reference videos to guide style, motion, and composition
  • • Clips up to 15 seconds — Longer than most open-source models
  • • Natural Language Editing — Edit existing videos by describing changes in text
  • • 27B parameters — One of the largest open-source video models

First/Last Frame Control in practice

Example: Set the first frame to “a person sitting in an office” and the last frame to “the same person running in a park.” Wan 2.7 generates the entire transition between them smoothly and coherently. Perfect for storytelling and creative transitions.

3

⚡ LTX-Video 2.3 — Speed and Resolution

O LTX-Video 2.3 stands out for two impressive numbers: Native 4K at 50fps e 18x faster than Wan 2.2. With 22 billion parameters, it is the fastest open-source model in its category.

LTX-Video 2.3 also generates native audio alongside video, following a trend started by Google Veo and now adopted by the open-source community.

When to choose LTX-Video

If you need 4K resolution and/or a high frame rate (slow motion, sports, nature), LTX-Video 2.3 is the best open-source option. For creative control (first/last frame), choose Wan 2.7.

4

🏭 Other relevant open-source models

HunyuanVideo (Tencent)

Tencent model focused on cinematic realism. Good for scenes with people and urban environments. Open source with a permissive license.

CogVideoX (Zhipu AI)

Focused on artistic, stylized video. Excellent for animation, motion graphics, and creative non-photorealistic content.

Open-Sora 2.0

Community project inspired by OpenAI's Sora. Focused on long clips and extended narratives. Still under active development.

☁️ Local vs Cloud

Run locally

  • ✅ No cost per generation
  • ✅ Full data privacy
  • ✅ No usage limits
  • ❌ Upfront hardware investment
  • ❌ Electricity consumption
  • ❌ Technical maintenance required

Run in the cloud

  • ✅ No hardware investment
  • ✅ Access to powerful GPUs (A100, H100)
  • ✅ On-demand scalability
  • ❌ Cost per hour/generation
  • ❌ Data in the provider's cloud
  • ❌ Network latency

Popular cloud services

RunPod ($0.39/h RTX 4090), Vast.ai (marketplace with variable pricing), Google Colab Pro ($9.99/month), Lambda Labs ($1.10/h A100). For ComfyUI in the cloud, RunPod and Vast.ai offer preconfigured templates.
