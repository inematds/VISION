Seedance 2.0 and Vidu Q3

Learn about the two video generators dominating the Video Arena Leaderboard: ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 and Shengshu Technology's Vidu Q3.

1

🌱 Seedance 2.0 — The Video Arena Leader

Developed by ByteDance (the same company behind TikTok), Seedance 2.0 reached the position #1 in the Video Arena Leaderboard, outperforming all competitors in visual quality, motion coherence, and prompt fidelity.

The model is especially strong at generating production-ready commercial video — in other words, videos that can be used directly in advertising campaigns without extensive post-production.

Why Is Seedance 2.0 Leading?

Three key factors: (1) training on the world's largest short-video dataset (TikTok/Douyin), (2) a diffusion architecture optimized for realistic human movement, and (3) a quality assurance system that rejects frames with artifacts before delivery.

Key Features of Seedance 2.0

  • • Native 1080p resolution — No upscaling required for professional use
  • • Ultra-realistic human movement — Hands, fingers, and facial expressions with impressive fidelity
  • • Temporal consistency — Objects and characters maintain a consistent appearance throughout the clip
  • • Text-to-video and image-to-video — Accepts text prompts or a reference image as input
  • • Up to 20 seconds per clip — Good enough for Reels, TikToks, and ad scenes
2

🎥 Vidu Q3 — Native Audio + Video

Vidu Q3, developed by Shengshu Technology, is the big surprise of 2026. It is the #1 in China and #2 globally in Video Arena. Its most impactful differentiator is generating native audio + video in a single output — you don’t need to add audio afterward.

While other models generate silent video, Vidu Q3 produces video with ambient sound effects, dialogue, and sounds synchronized with the visual action.

Native audio: what changes in practice

Imagine generating a video of a beach and automatically getting the sound of waves, seagulls, and wind. Or a video of coffee with the sound of an espresso machine and cups. This eliminates the sound design step for many projects.

3

⚡ TurboDiffusion — 200x Faster

One of the most impressive technical innovations in Vidu Q3 is the TurboDiffusion, an algorithm that speeds up video generation by up to 200x compared with traditional diffusion methods. In practice, this means:

  • • 8-second video in ~30 seconds — It used to take minutes
  • • Rapid iteration — Test multiple prompts without waiting through long processing times
  • • Reduced cost — Less GPU time = lower cost per generated video
4

🎨 Jimeng 3.0 and Seaweed 7B

In addition to Seedance, ByteDance maintains the ecosystem Jimeng 3.0 (formerly known as Dreamina)—an integrated visual creation platform that combines:

  • • Photorealistic image generation
  • • AI-assisted editing (inpainting, outpainting, style transfer)
  • • Direct integration with Seedance for an image-to-video workflow

O Seaweed 7B is ByteDance’s 7-billion-parameter foundation model for media generation. Despite its relatively compact size, it rivals much larger models thanks to distillation techniques and efficient training.

Access to Jimeng 3.0

Jimeng/Dreamina is available at jimeng.jianying.com. Some features may require VPN access or a Chinese account. Seedance 2.0 is more accessible internationally through seedance.ai.
