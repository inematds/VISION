Adobe Firefly Video and 30+ Models

Explore Adobe Firefly Video Editor with Prompt to Edit, reference-based camera control, Quick Cut, Firefly Boards, and access to more than 30 third-party models integrated into the Adobe ecosystem.

1

🔥 Adobe Firefly Video Editor

Adobe transformed Firefly from a simple image generator into a complete AI video editing platform. The Firefly Video Editor introduces features that no other traditional editor offers natively.

Adobe’s biggest differentiator isn’t just the quality of its generation, but seamless integration with the Creative Cloud ecosystem — Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop, and Illustrator.

Competitive advantage: IP Indemnification

Adobe offers intellectual property indemnification for content generated with Firefly. This means that if someone sues you for copyright infringement over content generated with Firefly, Adobe covers the legal costs. No other competitor offers this protection.

2

✏️ Prompt to Edit

O Prompt to Edit is the most revolutionary feature of Firefly Video Editor. Instead of using traditional editing controls, you simply describe what you want to change in natural language:

Prompt:

"Change the sky to a dramatic sunset with orange clouds"

Prompt:

"Add gently falling snow to the scene"

Prompt:

"Turn day into night while keeping the windows' artificial lighting"

Prompt:

"Remove the person in the background and fill in the scene"

How It Works Under the Hood

Prompt to Edit uses a combination of semantic segmentation (to understand “what” to edit), temporal inpainting (to maintain consistency across frames), and conditional generation (to apply the described change).

3

🎥 Camera Motion Control

O Camera Motion Control lets you extract camera movement from a reference video and apply it to a new AI-generated video. In practice:

  • 1. You upload a reference video with the desired camera movement
  • 2. Firefly analyzes and extracts the camera trajectory (pan, tilt, dolly, zoom)
  • 3. You describe a new scene via a prompt
  • 4. Firefly generates the new scene with the exact camera movement it extracted

Professional use case

Did you capture a perfect camera move on set, but the scene didn't turn out well? Extract the movement and apply it to an AI-generated scene. Or use classic cinematic camera moves as references for your projects.

4

✂️ Quick Cut and Firefly Boards

Quick Cut

O Quick Cut automatically analyzes your video and suggests cuts, transitions, and pacing based on the content and audio. Ideal for:

  • • Assemble the first cuts of long interviews
  • • Create event and talk highlights
  • • Generate short versions (15s, 30s, 60s) of long videos

Firefly Boards

The Firefly Boards are collaborative, AI-powered mood boards. You can:

  • • Collect visual references and generate variations with AI
  • • Collaborate with your team in real time
  • • Turn boards directly into video projects
  • • Maintain visual brand consistency across projects

🔗 Creative Cloud Integration

Adobe’s biggest advantage is its native integration with the professional tools the industry already uses:

Premiere Pro

Generate B-roll with Firefly directly on the timeline. Extend clips, change backgrounds, and add effects without leaving the editor.

After Effects

Integration for visual effects, AI-assisted rotoscoping, and advanced compositing with layers generated by Firefly.

Photoshop

Generative Fill and Expand to create reference frames and storyboards for your video projects.

Integration with Topaz Video AI to upscale generated videos from 1080p to 4K with professional quality.
