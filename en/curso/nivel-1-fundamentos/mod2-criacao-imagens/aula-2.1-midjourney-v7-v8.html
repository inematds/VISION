Midjourney V8.1 — From Prompt to Perfect Image

Master Midjourney V7 and explore V8 Alpha: interfaces, prompt engineering, Draft Mode, Omni Reference, and native video generation.

1

🎨 Midjourney V7 — Rebuilt from Scratch

Midjourney V7 wasn’t an incremental update — it was a complete rebuild of the model. Released in 2025, it brought fundamental changes to how Midjourney interprets and executes prompts.

Literal interpretation of prompts

V7 follows instructions precisely. If you ask for "3 cats," you get exactly 3 cats. Hands, fingers, and anatomy are rendered correctly. Text in images is legible and accurate.

Text rendering

For the first time, Midjourney reliably renders text within images. Perfect for logos, posters, covers, and marketing materials.

Draft Mode

Generates quick variations at lower resolution for creative exploration. 10x faster than normal mode. Ideal for testing ideas before generating at high resolution.

Omni Reference

Lets you use reference images to maintain consistency of characters, styles, and objects across multiple generations. Revolutionary for projects with a cohesive visual identity.

💡 V8 Alpha: Video Generation

V8 Alpha introduces native text-to-video generation of up to 10 seconds at 60fps. This positions Midjourney as a direct competitor to Kling and Runway in the video space, as well as in images. It’s still in alpha, but the initial results are promising.

🖥️ How to Use: Discord vs Web Interface

Discord (classic)

  • • /imagine command + prompt
  • • Access to all parameters (--ar, --stylize, --chaos etc.)
  • • Active community for inspiration
  • • Good for people who already use Discord

  • • Modern, intuitive visual interface

  • • Built-in editor for adjustments

✍️ Prompt Engineering for Midjourney

The art of writing good prompts is the most important skill for generating high-quality images. Here’s the recommended structure:

// Prompt structure

[SUBJECT] + [ACTION/SCENE] + [STYLE] + [LIGHTING] + [CAMERA] + [PARAMETERS]


// Practical example

A Brazilian woman in her 30s sitting at a modern coffee shop,

sipping espresso while reading on a tablet,

warm golden hour lighting through large windows,

photorealistic, Canon EOS R5 85mm f/1.4,

shallow depth of field --ar 16:9 --v 7

Essential parameters

  • • --ar 16:9 — aspect ratio (image proportions)
  • • --v 7 — model version (use 7 for stability)
  • • --stylize 100 — 0–1000, controls how much MJ “interprets” things artistically
  • • --chaos 20 — 0–100, variation between results
  • • --quality 2 — rendering quality (1 or 2)
  • • --no [item] — removes unwanted elements
