Finalization and Publishing

Export, optimize, and publish your videos on the major platforms with a strategy.

⏱️ ~60 minutes 📊 Beginner 🔄 Updated Apr/2026
1

📤 Export Settings

Exporting is the last step before publishing. The wrong settings can ruin a perfect video: a file that’s too large, low quality, or an incompatible format. Let’s learn the ideal settings for each situation.

Recommended settings by platform

Platform Resolution FPS Bitrate Codec
YouTube 1920x1080 (4K if possible) 30 or 60 10–20 Mbps H.264 / H.265
TikTok 1080x1920 30 5-10 Mbps H.264
Instagram Reels 1080x1920 30 5-10 Mbps H.264
LinkedIn 1920x1080 30 8–12 Mbps H.264
WhatsApp Status 720x1280 30 3-5 Mbps H.264

Resolution vs. Bitrate

Resolution defines the frame size (1080p, 4K). Bitrate defines the amount of data per second—the higher it is, the better the quality, but the larger the file. For social media, exporting in 4K is pointless if the platform compresses it to 1080p.

Exporting in CapCut

  1. Click Export (upper-right corner)
  2. Select the resolution (1080p recommended)
  3. Choose the frame rate (30fps for most cases)
  4. Adjust the quality (High or Custom)
  5. Format: MP4 (most universal)
  6. Set the name and save location
  7. Click Export and wait

🖼️ AI Thumbnails

The thumbnail is the most important element for attracting clicks. Studies show that 90% of the best-performing videos on YouTube have custom thumbnails. With AI, creating professional thumbnails is quick and easy.

AI thumbnail tools

Tool Method Price
Canva + AI Templates + AI image generation Freemium
Midjourney Generates unique images for thumbnails $10/month
Thumbnail.ai Automatic generation based on the title Freemium
Photopea + FLUX Free editor + AI image generation Free

Elements of an effective thumbnail

Expressive face

Strong expressions (surprise, joy) increase clicks by up to 38%

Short text (3-5 words)

Complements the title; never repeats it. Large, legible font.

Contrasting colors

Yellow, red, and blue stand out in the feed. Avoid pastel shades.

Clear composition

Viewers need to understand what it is in 1 second. Less is more.

Ideal thumbnail size

YouTube: 1280x720px (16:9), JPG or PNG format, max 2MB. TikTok: Frame from the video itself (the app selects it). Instagram: Square 1080x1080px or 9:16 for Reels.

🔍 Basic SEO for Video

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) for video helps more people find your content. A few simple optimizations can multiply your views.

SEO elements for each platform

Element YouTube TikTok Instagram
Title 60–70 chars, keyword at the beginning N/A (caption) N/A (caption)

Tags/Hashtags 10–15 relevant tags 3-5 trending hashtags 5-10 niche hashtags

Ask ChatGPT: "Generate a title, description, and 15 tags for YouTube about a video that teaches how to create images with AI using Midjourney. Audience: Brazilian beginners." AI will generate all the SEO-optimized content in seconds.

🚀 Multiplatform Publishing Workflow

To maximize your reach, publish on multiple platforms. Each has its own characteristics, but with a good strategy, you can adapt one piece of content for all channels.

Recommended publishing order

  1. YouTube (full video, 16:9): Publish the full version first. YouTube values original content and indexes the video on Google.
  2. TikTok (short version 9:16): Create a 15–60s version with the best moments. Publish within the first 2h after the YouTube video.
  3. Instagram Reels (short 9:16 version): Use the same version as TikTok or adapt it slightly. Publish it the same day.
  4. YouTube Shorts (short version, 9:16): Publish a different short version than the one on TikTok to avoid duplicate content detection.
  5. LinkedIn (professional version): If relevant to your niche, adapt it with a more formal tone.

Tip: Don’t publish exactly the same video on every platform

Adapt the content: change the hook (the first 3 seconds), adjust the caption text, and use hashtags specific to each platform. This improves algorithm performance and avoids penalties for duplicate content.

📊 Basic Analytics

Publishing is only half the work. Analyzing the results is essential for continuous improvement. See the most important metrics for each platform.

Essential metrics

Metric What it measures Initial goal
CTR (Click-Through Rate) % of people who click after seeing the thumbnail > 5%
Average retention % of the video people watch > 40%
Engagement rate Likes + comments + shares > 3%
Viewing time Total minutes watched Growing month over month
Conversion rate Sign-ups / new followers per video Increasing

AI analysis

Paste the YouTube Analytics data into ChatGPT and ask: "Analyze performance data from my last 10 videos and tell me the patterns, what's working, and suggest improvements." AI identifies trends that would be difficult to spot manually.
