🎬 CapCut in 2026: Overview

CapCut has evolved from a simple editor into a complete AI-powered editing platform. In 2026, it offers features that were once exclusive to professional software like Adobe Premiere and DaVinci Resolve, but with the ease of use that made it the world's most popular editor.

Why CapCut?

Free, cross-platform (desktop, web, and mobile), native integration with TikTok and Instagram, and now with dozens of built-in AI features that automate repetitive editing tasks.

Key AI features in CapCut (2026)

Background Remover Remove video backgrounds with one click Free
Auto-Captions Automatically generates synchronized captions Free
AI Script Generator Suggests scripts based on trends Pro
Smart Resize Adapts videos to different formats while maintaining framing Free

🖥️ Interface and Initial Setup

Before you start editing, let's set up CapCut properly for the best workflow.

Step by step: Initial setup

  1. Download: Access capcut.com and download the desktop version (Windows or Mac)
  2. Account: Create a free account (or sign in with your TikTok/Google account)
  3. Project settings: Go to Settings and set the default resolution to 1080p
  4. Shortcuts: Enable keyboard shortcuts in Settings > Shortcuts
  5. Working folder: Create an organized folder for your VISION projects

Organization Tip

Create a folder structure like this: VISION/Projeto-Nome/assets/, VISION/Projeto-Nome/exports/. This makes it easier to find your files and keep everything organized.

Getting to know the interface

Area Function
Media Panel (left) Import and organize media, text, effects, and transitions
Preview (top center) Real-time video preview
Timeline (bottom) Timeline for organizing and trimming clips
Properties (right) Properties of the selected element (position, scale, opacity)

⚡ Auto-Cut: Intelligent Automatic Editing

Auto-Cut is CapCut's most revolutionary feature for content creators. It analyzes your video and automatically removes silences, pauses, and moments without speech, saving hours of manual editing.

How to Use Auto-Cut

  1. Import your video into the timeline
  2. Select the clip on the timeline
  3. Click AI Tools > Auto-Cut
  4. Adjust the sensitivity (recommended: 70-80% for spoken content)
  5. Click Apply and review the cuts
  6. Manually adjust the points that were inaccurate

Results in Practice

A 10-minute video with natural speech pauses can automatically be shortened to 6-7 minutes, resulting in much more dynamic and engaging content for the viewer.

🎨 Smart Templates and AI Effects

Smart Templates are preconfigured templates that automatically adapt to your content. Just add your media and CapCut adjusts the timing, transitions, and effects.

Popular Template Categories

  • Product/E-commerce: Product presentations with zoom and highlights
  • Vlog/Lifestyle: Smooth transitions and animated text
  • Educational: Large captions, markers, and highlights
  • Reels/TikTok: Fast transitions, dynamic text, trending music
  • Corporate: Clean, professional, with lower thirds

Featured AI Effects

Style Transfer

Transforms the video's aesthetic to look like anime, oil painting, watercolor, and more.

AI Enhance

Automatically improves the quality, lighting, and color of low-quality video footage.

Face Beautify

Skin smoothing, facial lighting adjustment, and red-eye correction.

Motion Blur AI

Adds realistic motion blur for transitions and cinematic effects.

📥 Importing AI-Generated Content

Throughout the course, you generated images, videos, and audio with AI. Now let's learn how to import and organize these assets in CapCut to create complete productions.

Supported Formats

Type Formats Source Tool
Images PNG, JPG, WebP Midjourney, FLUX, DALL-E
Videos MP4, MOV, WebM Kling 3.0, Runway, Pika
Audio MP3, WAV, M4A ElevenLabs, Suno

Recommended workflow

  1. Generate all the necessary assets (images, videos, audio)
  2. Organize into subfolders: /imagens, /videos, /audio
  3. Import everything at once into CapCut (drag the entire folder)
  4. Use the media panel's search feature to find assets quickly

📱 Social Media Formats

Each platform has its ideal format. CapCut makes adaptation easier with its Smart Resize feature, but it's important to plan the format from the start of editing.

Platform Aspect ratio Resolution Ideal duration
TikTok 9:16 (vertical) 1080 x 1920 15s - 3min
Instagram Reels 9:16 (vertical) 1080 x 1920 15s - 90s
YouTube 16:9 (horizontal) 1920 x 1080 8min - 20min
YouTube Shorts 9:16 (vertical) 1080 x 1920 15s - 60s
Instagram Feed 1:1 (square) 1080 x 1080 15s - 60s
LinkedIn 1:1 or 16:9 1080 x 1080 / 1920 x 1080 30s - 5min

Smart Resize: One video, every format

Edit first in 16:9 format (YouTube). Then use CapCut Smart Resize to automatically convert to 9:16 and 1:1. AI repositions important elements in the frame, keeping the content centered and visible in all formats.

🛠️ Practical Exercise

We'll create a short product introduction video using AI-generated content.

Step-by-step exercise guide

  1. Create the project: Open CapCut and create a new 1080x1920 project (9:16 vertical)
  2. Import assets: Use 3-5 AI-generated images (from previous lessons)
  3. Put together the sequence: Drag the images to the timeline, each one 3-4 seconds long
  4. Add transitions: Add smooth transitions between images
  5. Apply AI Effects: Test Style Transfer on at least one clip
  6. Add text: Add a title at the beginning and a call to action at the end
  7. Add music: Use CapCut's free audio library or a song from Suno
  8. Export: Export in 1080p to share
