🎓 Module 5 • Lesson 1

Project Planning

Define the concept, create the script, and plan all the assets for your final project.

⏱️ ~60 minutes 📊 Beginner 🔄 Updated Apr/2026
1

🎯 Defining the Concept

The Level 1 final project is to create a 30-60 second promotional video for a product or service, using only AI tools. Before producing any assets, we need to plan carefully.

What is the final project?

A complete promotional video combining: AI-generated images (Midjourney/FLUX), AI video clips (Kling 3.0/Runway), synthetic narration (ElevenLabs v3), background music (Suno v5), all edited in CapCut. The result should look like it was produced by a professional team.

Choosing your product or service

Choose something you know or are interested in. It can be real or fictional. Examples:

Physical Product

Headphones, sneakers, cosmetics, food, gadget

Digital Product

App, online course, e-book, template, SaaS tool

Service

Consulting, design, photography, marketing, coffee

Personal brand

Personal presentation, portfolio, YouTube channel

Defining the target audience

Answer these questions before continuing:

  • Who is the audience? (Age, interests, pain points, desires)
  • Where will you watch it? (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn)
  • What action do you want people to take? (Buy, follow, learn more, subscribe)
  • What’s the tone? (Fun, professional, inspiring, urgent)
  • What sets it apart? (What makes this product/service special)
2

📝 Creating the Script with ChatGPT

The script is the backbone of your video. We’ll use ChatGPT to create a professional, structured script.

Prompt for ChatGPT

Crie um roteiro para um vídeo promocional de [30/60] segundos
para [descreva o produto/serviço].

Público-alvo: [descreva]
Plataforma principal: [TikTok/Instagram/YouTube]
Tom: [divertido/profissional/inspirador]

O roteiro deve incluir:
1. Hook (primeiros 3 segundos) - algo que prenda atenção
2. Problema que o produto resolve
3. Apresentação da solução
4. Benefícios principais (máximo 3)
5. Call-to-action (chamada para ação)

Formato: inclua timestamps, texto da narração e descrição
visual de cada cena.

Script structure (30 seconds)

Time Section Goal Duration
0-3s Hook Capture attention immediately 3 seconds
3-8s Problem Identify your audience's pain point 5 seconds
8-15s Solution Present the product/service 7 seconds
15-25s Benefits Show 2-3 main advantages 10 seconds
25-30s CTA Tell the viewer what to do 5 seconds

The 3-second rule

On social media, you have exactly 3 seconds to convince someone to keep watching. The hook is the most important part of the video. Use provocative questions, surprising data, or striking images.

3

🎨 AI Storyboarding

The storyboard is a visual representation of each scene in your video. We’ll use Midjourney to quickly create visual references for each scene planned in the script.

Creating the storyboard

  1. List the scenes: Based on the script, identify each visually distinct scene
  2. Describe each scene: Write a short visual description for each one
  3. Generate references: Use Midjourney to generate one reference image per scene
  4. Organize: Put the images in order with descriptions and timestamps

Storyboard prompt for Midjourney

Use this format: "storyboard frame, [descrição da cena], [estilo visual], clean composition, --ar 16:9 --v 7". Generate quickly at low resolution (--quality 0.5) to save credits, since these are only references.

Storyboard example (headphone video)

Scene Time Visual Voice-over
1 - Hook 0-3s Close-up of the headphones with dramatic lighting "What if you could listen to music like never before?"
2 - Problem 3-8s Person on the subway with noise "External noise ruining your focus?"
3 - Solution 8-15s Person putting on headphones, peace "SonicPro X: intelligent noise cancellation"
4 - Benefits 15-25s Assembly: gym, café, office "40 hours of battery life. Premium comfort. Award-winning design."
5 - CTA 25-30s Product + logo + link "Get yours. Link in bio."

🧰 Tool Selection

Depending on your project, some tools will be more suitable than others. Here's a guide to choosing the right tool for each need.

Need Best option Free alternative
Photorealistic Images Midjourney v7 FLUX (via Replicate)
Images with text FLUX Ideogram
Realistic video Kling 3.0 Pika (free version)
Artistic video Runway Gen-4.5 Pika
Natural narration ElevenLabs v3 ElevenLabs (free tier)
Background music Suno v5 Suno (free tier) / CapCut library

Time planning

Set aside at least 2h for asset generation (next lesson), 2h for assembly, and 1h for finalization. Generating AI images and videos may require multiple attempts to achieve the desired result, so don't underestimate the time.
