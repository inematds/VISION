🎓 Module 5 • Lesson 2

Asset Production

Generate all the visual elements, audio, and videos for your project using AI tools.

⏱️ ~120 minutes 📊 Beginner 🔄 Updated Apr/2026
1

🖼️ Generating Product Images

Images are the visual foundation of your video. Let's generate high-quality product images in different settings and angles using Midjourney and FLUX.

Images Needed (Typical for 30s)

Scene Image type Base prompt
Hero shot Featured Product, Premium Background "product photography, [product], dramatic lighting, dark background"
Context Product in Everyday Use "person using [product], lifestyle photography, natural light"
Detail Close-up of details and textures "macro photography, [product detail], sharp focus"
Scenario Background/environment for replacement "[scene description], wide shot, cinematic, --ar 16:9"

Midjourney vs. FLUX: When to Use Each

  • • Midjourney: Best for aesthetic, artistic, and product images with dramatic lighting
  • • FLUX: Best for images with legible text, specific compositions, and photorealism
  • • Tip: Generate 3-4 variations of each image and choose the best one

Image production workflow

  1. Review your storyboard and identify each image needed
  2. Write prompts based on the script’s visual descriptions
  3. Generate 3-4 variations for each scene in Midjourney
  4. Select the best ones and upscale them (maximum resolution)
  5. If needed, use FLUX for images that need text
  6. Save all the images in the folder /assets/imagens/
  7. Name them in an organized way: cena01-hero.png, cena02-contexto.png

🎥 Generating Video Clips

AI-generated video clips add dynamism to the project. Use Kling 3.0 for realistic videos and Runway for more artistic effects.

AI video strategies for the project

Image-to-Video

Use the images generated in Midjourney as a starting point. Upload them to Kling 3.0 and animate the scene with subtle movements: slow motion, zoom, object movement.

Text-to-Video

For more dynamic scenes, describe the action in text and let AI generate the full video. Ideal for transition scenes and B-roll.

Tips for better results

Tool Ideal duration Key Tip
Kling 3.0 5-10 seconds Describe camera movements in the prompt (pan, zoom, orbit)
Runway Gen-4.5 4–8 seconds Use a reference image for visual consistency
Pika 3-5 seconds Great for short, eye-catching creative effects

Don't try to generate everything as video

For a 30-second video, 2-3 AI video clips are enough. The rest can be made up of still images with camera movements (zoom, pan) added in CapCut. Mixing animated images with video clips is the most efficient strategy.

3

🎙️ Recording the Voiceover with ElevenLabs v3

Narration brings your video to life. ElevenLabs v3 (ElevenMusic) (ElevenMusic) generates incredibly natural Brazilian Portuguese voices with fine control over emotion, pacing, and intonation.

Step-by-step narration guide

  1. Go to the ElevenLabs > Text to Speech
  2. Select a Brazilian Portuguese voice (test 3-4 different voices)
  3. Paste the narration text from your script
  4. Adjust the settings:
    • Stability: 50-70% (more natural variation)
    • Clarity: 75–85% (clear voice, but not robotic)
    • Style: Adjust to match the video’s tone
  5. Generate the narration and listen carefully
  6. Adjust words or phrases that sound strange
  7. Export as MP3 or WAV (high quality)

Tip: Pauses and emphasis

Use strategic punctuation in the text to control the narration: Commas = short pauses, Points = long pauses, UPPERCASE = emphasis, ... = dramatic pause. Example: "What if I told you... this costs LESS than 50 reais?"

4

🎵 Creating Background Music with Suno v5

Music sets the video’s emotional atmosphere. Suno v5.5 generates original music that fits your project perfectly.

Background music prompt

Estilo: [energético/calmo/inspirador/corporativo]
BPM: [80-100 para calmo, 120-140 para energético]
Instrumentos: [piano, synth, beats eletrônicos, violão]
Mood: [motivacional, sofisticado, divertido, dramático]
Duração: [30s ou 60s]
Sem vocais (instrumental only)

Music styles by video type

Video type Musical style BPM
Tech/Gadget Product Electronic, futuristic, strong beats 120-130
Beauty Product Soft, elegant, piano + synth pad 90-110
Food/Drink Cheerful, acoustic, guitar + light percussion 100-120
Corporate/SaaS Motivational, light orchestral music + beats 110-125
Fashion/Lifestyle Lo-fi, chill, mellow beats 85-100

Background music volume

Background music should be -15dB to -20dB below the narration. In CapCut, set the music track volume to 15-25% of the total. The music should be felt, not consciously heard—it complements the narration and never competes with it.

📂 Organizing the Assets

With all the assets generated, organize them clearly before moving on to assembly.

Recommended folder structure

projeto-final/
├── imagens/
│   ├── cena01-hero-produto.png
│   ├── cena02-contexto-uso.png
│   ├── cena03-detalhe.png
│   └── cena04-cenario-fundo.png
├── videos/
│   ├── cena01-intro-animado.mp4
│   ├── cena03-transicao.mp4
│   └── cena04-montagem.mp4
├── audio/
│   ├── narracao-completa.mp3
│   └── musica-fundo.mp3
├── exports/
│   └── (vídeos finalizados aqui)
└── roteiro.txt

Asset Quality Checklist

🖼️
Images: Minimum resolution of 1920x1080, no visible artifacts, consistent style across scenes
🎥
Videos: Minimum 720p, no glitches, smooth motion, appropriate duration for the scene
🎙️
Narration: Clean audio, free of noise, with correct pronunciation and a tone suited to the project
🎵
Music: Duration compatible with the video, no vocals (instrumental), right mood
