⚡ Key Features of Kling 3.0

Audio + Native Video

Kling 3.0’s most important differentiator: it generates video WITH synchronized audio. Ambient sounds, dialogue, sound effects—all generated along with the video. Multiple languages are supported. In many cases, there’s no longer any need to add audio separately.

Multi-Shot Storyboard

Create sequences of multiple shots while maintaining character and setting consistency. Define the complete visual narrative, and Kling generates each scene connected to the previous one. Perfect for short films and commercials.

Up to 15 Seconds per Clip

Generates videos up to 15 seconds long in a single generation. It may seem short, but that’s twice as long as most competitors. With Multi-Shot, you can chain multiple clips to create longer videos.

Kling Image 3.0

In addition to video, Kling also generates images in 2K and 4K resolution. Useful for creating visual references before generating the video.

Kling O1 — Unified Model

Kuaishou’s unified multimodal model combines text, image, and video understanding and generation in a single system. It’s still in development, but promises to unify the entire creative pipeline.

🎬 Step by Step: Your First Video with Kling

1. Go to klingai.com and log in

Use the account you created in Lesson 1.4. Go to the video generation section.

2. Choose the generation mode

Text-to-Video (text to video) or Image-to-Video (image to video). To get started, use Text-to-Video.

3. Write your prompt

Be descriptive: "A woman in a white dress walks through a sunflower field at golden hour, camera slowly follows from behind, cinematic, warm tones". Turn on the audio option if you want ambient sounds.

4. Configure the parameters

Duration (5s, 10s, 15s), resolution (720p or 1080p), aspect ratio (16:9, 9:16, 1:1). Use 9:16 for Instagram/TikTok posts.

5. Generate and evaluate

Click Generate and wait (1-3 minutes). Evaluate the result and iterate on the prompt if needed. Download the video when you’re satisfied.

⚠️ Tips for better results

  • • Describe the camera movement (pan, zoom, tracking shot)
  • • Specify the lighting (golden hour, studio lighting, neon)
  • • Include the desired style (cinematic, documentary, commercial)
  • • For Image-to-Video, use images generated with Midjourney for better consistency
