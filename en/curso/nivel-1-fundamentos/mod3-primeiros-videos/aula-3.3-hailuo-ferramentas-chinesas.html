Hailuo and Affordable Chinese Tools

Discover the Chinese AI video tools revolutionizing the market with aggressive pricing and surprising quality: Hailuo, Vidu, Seedance, and Wan.

1

🌊 Hailuo / MiniMax — The Cheapest on the Market

Hailuo, developed by MiniMax, shocked the market with absurdly low prices without compromising quality:

$0.28 per video in 1080p

Yes, less than R$1.50 per video in Full HD. That's 10-50x cheaper than the Western competition.

Hailuo 2.3 — Micro-expressions

The latest version delivers incredibly realistic micro-expressions. Eyebrows, lips, eyes—everything moves naturally. It also supports native anime styles, making it ideal for Japanese animation content.

When to use Hailuo

Ideal for: high-volume production, concept testing, social media content, projects with limited budgets, and anime content. The quality is good enough for most social media uses.

2

🎭 Vidu Q3 — #1 in China

Vidu Q3 reached the #1 position on Chinese platforms with a unique approach:

Audio + Native Video

Like Kling 3.0, Vidu generates video with synchronized audio. It's strong at dialogue scenes, facial expressiveness, and human interactions.

Focus on Expressiveness

Vidu stands out at generating videos with emotion — expressive faces, natural gestures, and interactions between people. Ideal for narrative content and storytelling.

3

🏆 Seedance 2.0 — #1 on Video Arena

Developed by ByteDance (the company behind TikTok), Seedance 2.0 took first place on the Video Arena Leaderboard:

What is the Video Arena Leaderboard?

Similar to Chatbot Arena for LLMs, Video Arena is a platform where users evaluate videos generated by different models in blind comparisons (blind tests). Seedance 2.0 is currently #1, outperforming Kling, Runway, and all the others.

Exceptional quality

It benefits from ByteDance’s expertise in video processing (TikTok). Natural movement, vibrant colors, and temporal consistency are its strengths.

Expanding access

Originally available only in China, Seedance is expanding international access. Check availability in your region at seedance.ai.

4

🔓 Wan 2.7 — Alibaba's Open-Source Model

Wan, developed by Alibaba, is the leading open-source video model:

Completely open source

Code and model weights available on Hugging Face. You can run it locally, modify it, and integrate it into any pipeline without licensing costs.

Clips up to 15 seconds

Generates videos up to 15 seconds long in good quality. Supports instruction-based editing — describe in text what you want to change in an existing video.

Who it's for

Developers, researchers, and technical creators who want full control. Requires a powerful GPU (16GB+ VRAM) to run locally. Alternative: use it via API on platforms such as Replicate.
